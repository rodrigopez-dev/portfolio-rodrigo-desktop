# Dock icon sources

- After Effects, Photoshop, Illustrator: [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), [MIT license](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/blob/master/LICENSE).
- Mail, Notes, Photos: [macOS 26 icon exports](https://github.com/zagnut531/macOS-26-Icons), exported from a Mac by the repository author. These images depict Apple app icons and remain Apple artwork; the repository is a source of the export, not an official Apple distribution.
- PowerPoint and Instagram: current app icon artwork from their [Apple App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/instagram/id389801252) and [PowerPoint listing](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/microsoft-powerpoint/id462062816) through Apple's public artwork feed. The Instagram artwork is a JPEG and is stored as `instagram-appstore.jpg`.

- `wallpaper-v4-mobile.jpg`: center crop of `wallpaper-v4.jpeg` (2160 x 2880 region, resized to 1800 x 2400) used only on screens up to 700 px wide; same framing as the original with `cover`.

Product names and logos belong to their respective owners.
